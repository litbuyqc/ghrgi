The shopping method

How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet for better research.

A useful list explains why an item belongs on it. Whether you are researching Oopbuy finds or building your own wardrobe tracker, record enough information to compare alternatives fairly.

Keep the original product link, a checked date and your notes together. An old list entry should prompt a fresh check of the current listing.

Start your category research ↗

  1. Choose a specific category

    Begin with a concrete need: everyday sneakers, a layering hoodie, a lightweight jacket or a finishing accessory.

  2. Record comparable details

    Save the item name, seller, listed material, measurements, currency and product URL. Separate listing facts from your own impressions.

  3. Review photos and measurements

    Inspect the sole, seams, closures, print placement or jewelry finish where relevant. Compare item measurements with something you already own.

  4. Compare the full cost and terms

    Check available shipping information, possible additional charges, return conditions and any service fees with the relevant provider.

  5. Recheck before deciding

    Confirm the selected variant, availability and current price. A discovery list does not reserve stock or guarantee the destination listing.

Direct answers

Before you follow a find.

Clear answers to common Oopbuy spreadsheet questions, with a focus on what this page actually provides.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize links and comparison notes for shopping research. Its contents vary by creator, so check each destination listing for current details.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS editorial guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not provide an Oopbuy account, checkout or official service.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a category directory and comparison guide, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. You can use its checklist to organize your own product research.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with a garment or pair of shoes that already fits. Check the measurement units and whether the chart describes body measurements or item measurements.

Do listing photos prove brand authenticity?

No. Photos and brand names alone do not establish authenticity. Review the seller, provenance, product details and applicable return terms before deciding.

Are prices, stock and shipping costs guaranteed?

No. Confirm current availability, currency, shipping options and any destination-specific charges with the relevant provider before purchasing.
